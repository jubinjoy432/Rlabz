About Us

RLabZ is the student innovation and development laboratory under the Rajagiri Centre for Incubation, Department of Computer Science, Rajagiri College of Social Sciences. We empower students to work on real-world software projects, collaborate with industry partners, and transform innovative ideas into practical digital solutions.

2013 Founded
50+ Projects Delivered
200+ Students Trained
20+ Clients Served
25+ Technologies

Vision

To become a leading student-driven innovation laboratory that nurtures creativity, entrepreneurship, and technological excellence while delivering impactful digital solutions for society.

Mission

  • Foster practical learning through real-world software projects.
  • Encourage innovation and research.
  • Build industry-ready software engineers.
  • Deliver quality digital products for organizations.
  • Promote collaboration between academia and industry.
