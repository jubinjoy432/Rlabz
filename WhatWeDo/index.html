Initiative

Fostering Innovation

RLabz is an initiative under Rajagiri Centre for Incubation by the school of Computer Science. We maintain strong partnerships with Technopark TBI and Kerala State Industry Development Corporation (KSIDC), supporting both IT and non-IT startups in their journey towards excellence.

Vision

Relentless Excellence

Established through the foresight of the CMI, Rajagiri College of Social Sciences embodies an indefatigable spirit. The School of Computer Science upholds the axiomatic principle of 'Relentlessly towards Excellence' in every endeavor.

Incubation Centre

Rajagiri Incubation Centre

The Rajagiri Incubation Centre (RIC) provides RSET alumni and students with world-class facilities to design, develop, and deploy innovative ideas and concepts. Supported by a dedicated team of experienced faculty members who provide constant encouragement for new and developmental initiatives.
